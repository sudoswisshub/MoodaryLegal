Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Moodary has no accounts and no servers receiving your entries. See the Privacy Policy.

I switched phones. How do I get my entries back?

Entries are stored on your device, so restore your new phone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one. Premium users can also export CSV, JSON or PDF from Settings.

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open Moodary, open the Premium screen and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID as the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Moodary and tap Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I export or delete my data?

Premium users can export from Settings. To delete everything, delete the app from your device.

Apple Health, calendar or microphone is not working

Open iOS Settings → Moodary (and Settings → Privacy & Security → Health) and make sure the permission is enabled, then restart the app.

Widgets or Apple Watch are not updating

Open the iPhone app once to refresh, then check that the widget is added again (long-press the Home Screen) and that the Watch app is installed from the Watch app on iPhone.

Reminders are not showing

Check Settings → Notifications → Moodary, make sure reminders are enabled in the app, and that Focus modes or quiet hours are not silencing them.

Is Moodary medical advice?

No. Moodary is for self-reflection only. If you are struggling, please reach out to a healthcare professional or a crisis line.
