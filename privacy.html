Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Summary: Moodary has no account and no server that receives your entries. Your mood data stays on your device. We do not track you, show ads, or sell or share your data.

Introduction

Moodary (“we,” “our,” or “us”) values your privacy. This policy explains how your information is handled when you use the Moodary mood tracking app on iPhone and Apple Watch.

Information We Collect

Moodary does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. Everything you enter (moods, notes, tags, goals, journal reflections) is stored locally on your device and remains under your control.

Permissions and optional features

Each of these is optional, requested only when you use the related feature, and can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings.

PermissionWhy we askWhat happens to the data
Apple Health (HealthKit)To compare mood with steps, heart rate, sleep and exercise.Read on your device only. Never sent to us, never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with data brokers. Not written to iCloud by Moodary.

How We Use Your Information

Because your data stays on your device, we do not use it for advertising, analytics, profiling, or tracking. Moodary does not use any third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Apple Watch, widgets and sync

Mood data is shared between the iPhone app, Home Screen widgets and the Apple Watch app on your own devices so they stay up to date. This happens locally through Apple’s system frameworks.

Purchases

Premium purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details or Apple ID. Apple provides the app with confirmation of your purchase status so that Premium features can be unlocked.

Data Storage, Backup and Security

  • All entries are stored locally on your device and protected by iOS device security. You can add a Face ID lock inside the app.
  • No accounts are required to use Moodary.
  • Optional iCloud device backup is controlled by Apple and your device settings.
  • You can export your data (Premium) or delete everything by deleting the app.

Your Choices and Rights

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on a server. You control your data directly in the app and in iOS Settings. If you are in the EU/EEA, UK or Switzerland, you have rights under applicable data protection law; contact us if you have any questions about them.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, or transfer any information to third parties.

Children’s Privacy

Moodary is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version here and update the “Last updated” date.
